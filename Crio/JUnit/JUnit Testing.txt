JUnit Testing

What is JUnit Testing?
Annotations: @Test, @DisplayName, @BeforeEach, @AfterEach, @BeforeAll, @AfterAll. @RepeatedTest, @Disabled
Junit Assertions: assertEquals, assertTrue, assertFalse, assertNull, assertNotNull, assertThrows(used with exceptions)

@Test
@DisplayName("Test Subtraction Overflow of Two Doubles")
void testSubtractionOverflowForDoubles(){
 	//Assert
        Assertions.assertThrows(ArithmeticException.class,new Executable(){
            @Override
            public void execute() throws Throwable{
                standardCalculator.subtract(-Double.MAX_VALUE,Double.MAX_VALUE);
            }
        });
}
