	If many elements were 0 between j and i and you swap i and j and increment j, then also j remains at 0.

8. Container With Most Water
	We have two possibilities: 
		1. Move away from greater height
		2. Move away from smaller height

	If we move away from greater height, then if: 
		1. Next element is smaller: area will reduce
		2. Next element is equal: area will reduce
		3. Next element is greater: Still area will reduce because smaller height will decide area.  
		
	If we move away from smaller height, then if: 
		1. Next element is smaller: area will reduce
		2. Next element is equal: area will reduce
		3. Next element is greater: Area will increase if the difference is more than 1. 

	Hence, it is better to move away from smaller height.
