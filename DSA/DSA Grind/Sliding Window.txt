Sliding Window 
1. Maximum Sum Subarray of Size K (LeetCode 643 – Maximum Average Subarray I)
	→ Classic fixed-size window, compute sum efficiently.

2. Minimum Size Subarray Sum (LeetCode 209)
	→ Variable-size window, shrink from left when sum ≥ target.

3. Longest Substring Without Repeating Characters (LeetCode 3)
	→ HashSet/Map with sliding window.

4. Binary Subarray With Sum (LeetCode 930)
	It is difficult to calculate sum with exact goal. 
	Instead you can calculate: (Sum <= goal) - (Sum <= goal - 1) 

5. Max  Consecutive Ones III
	At most k zero flips → sliding window with a counter or use k as a counter.
